  1. npj PO
    Bio-primed machine learning to enhance discovery of relevant biomarkers
    David M. Henke, Alexander Renwick, James Zoeller, and 6 more authors
    npj Precision Oncology, 2025
    Abs DOI Bib HTML

    BioPrimeLASSO incorporates protein-protein interaction network scores from STRING DB into the LASSO regularization penalty. Applied to 453 cancer targets across 1,048 DepMap cell lines, it recovered GAB2 as an EGFR dependency biomarker missed by standard approaches.
