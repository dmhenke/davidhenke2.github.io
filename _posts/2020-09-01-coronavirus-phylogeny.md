This article examines SARS-CoV-2 spike protein sequences collected from BCM isolates and healthcare workers during the early pandemic. Using phylogenetic reconstruction across nine isolates, we identified five unique spike sequences — offering a window into local viral diversity at Baylor College of Medicine.

The full interactive analysis is available at: [dmhenke.github.io/Article_Coronavirus_Phylogeny](https://dmhenke.github.io/Article_Coronavirus_Phylogeny/)
