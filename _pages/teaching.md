I advise students and collaborators on data-driven research design, statistical
modeling, and reproducible analysis in R, and I serve as a peer reviewer for
_npj_. Much of this work happens inside collaborations rather than the
classroom — guiding experimental design, framing analyses, and helping teams
move from a statistically significant result to a biologically meaningful one.

If you are a student or collaborator looking for input on study design or
computational methods, I am glad to talk.
