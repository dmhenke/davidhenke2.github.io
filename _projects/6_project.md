Multifunction Solar Utah is a research-first pre-feasibility study of solar infrastructure sited on Utah's public waters and working lands, where a single capital investment produces both electrons and consumptively-saved water. The work reframes Utah's two simultaneous constraints — GW-scale load growth and a terminal-lake water crisis — as one joint optimization problem.

## Features

- Portfolio framing: marginal cost of a saved acre-foot and a delivered megawatt-hour, jointly
- Little Dell Reservoir floating-PV pre-feasibility site plan, with culinary-source design constraints
- Great Salt Lake tiered site assessment: playa ground-mount above the 4,200 ft lake-recovery contour
- Agrivoltaics as a water-generation technology, quantified against evapotranspiration reduction
- Phased strategy playbook from literature synthesis through funded demonstration
